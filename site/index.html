Cairn

A trail for every task.

Juggling Vibe Coding projects and app windows? Cairn is a quiet macOS companion that leaves floating notes when Codex, Claude Code, OpenClaw, or Hermes finishes a task. No notifications. No stolen focus. Open one to jump back to where it ran.

Download for macOS macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon · Free and open source · Local only, no telemetry
2

Notes float beside a small stack of stones. Click the stones to expand or collapse; drag them to a quieter corner of the screen.

Never interrupts

Cairn has no window and asks for nothing. It reports that something finished; it never demands that you respond.

Everything stacks

Notes queue in one fixed rhythm. Fifty finished turns are scannable in a single glance, and nothing reflows.

Traces back

Click a note and Cairn retraces its steps — back to the exact terminal tab, editor window, or session where the turn ran. When it can't find that window, it opens the app instead; failing that, it reveals the folder in Finder.

Stay focused

You don't have to hold ten tasks in your head at once — Cairn remembers where each one lives. Stay with the one in front of you; the rest is waiting, ready to be traced back to.
